## What You Actually Do in Probably Stolen

The premise is simple enough to explain in one sentence and deep enough to eat a weekend: you run a shop on a dystopian space station, and you need to earn enough to buy out the property before climbing rent forces you out. Every customer at the counter could be a security informant fishing for evidence, a revolutionary hunting leverage, or a fallen elite testing whether you are useful. Approve the sale, refuse it, report it, or look away — someone is always taking notes.

Where the game opens up is in what you choose to sell. Your starting trade determines your profit curve and, more importantly, the specific flavor of trouble that follows you. Margins and consequences scale together, which is why picking a product line is really picking which risk you want to monetize.

### Starting Trades and Their Hidden Costs

| Starting trade | What you move | Primary risk to manage |

| Chemist | Profitable chemicals | Addiction fallout and closely guarded recipes |

| Fence | Discounted stolen goods | Raids and Security attention |
| Hydroponic farmer | Grown crops | Supply swings and spoilage |
| Rock bottom | Whatever you can afford | Everything, all at once |

Beyond selling, the loop includes appraising rare items before you overpay, haggling for better deals, running production chains and machines, unlocking licenses to reach the underground market, and building faction relationships that unlock exclusive stock. You can also cut quality, relabel counterfeits as genuine, and bribe your way out of a bust.

### Practical Tips for Your First Shifts

- **Inspect before you buy, not after.** Suppliers overstate value constantly, and contraband often hides defects you only notice once the deal is closed.
- **Keep rent as a hard floor.** Confiscated stock or one bad purchase can leave you short on the next payment, so never spend down to your last credit.
- **Diversify slowly.** Additional jobs — water merchant, hydroponic farmer, organ dealer — add revenue but also add inspection surfaces.
- **Track who is watching.** Reputation buys access and protection, but it also paints a target on your back.
- **Watch for augmentation tells.** The rogue AI chip subplot means some customers are not what they appear, and misreading the signs carries a price.
